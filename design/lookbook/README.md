# Homepage redesign: "The Lookbook"

Design reference for the planned public homepage redesign. Nothing in this
folder is served by the site (it is outside `public/`).

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `lookbook-mockup.html` | Interactive mockup (v7). Open it in a browser; make the window narrow to see the phone layout. Gallery photos load from `public/gallery/`. |
| `videos/lookbook-iphone.mp4` | iPhone scroll-through showing the animations. |
| `videos/lookbook-desktop.mp4` | Desktop scroll-through showing the animations. |
| `boards/lookbook-v7.png` | Chosen direction, desktop and iPhone side by side. |
| `boards/concept-a-polish-bar.png`, `boards/concept-c-neighborhood.png` | The two alternative directions that were not picked, kept for reference. |
| `img/team.jpg` | Team photo used in the "family business" section. |

## What the design includes

- Nail art leads, with the other services named in the hero
  (Nails · Pedicures · Waxing · Brows & Lashes) and a "Beyond nails" section.
- Nail art pitched broadly: from a photo, from an idea, or freestyle.
- Phone number tappable in the header and in a sticky Call / Book bar on phones.
- "A family business, not a chain" section with the team photo.
- Reviews show their opening lines with "Read full review".
- Motion: headline words rise in, then "your way." paints on separately.
  Section titles do the same (the rose italic phrase paints on last), but only
  once they are well on screen, so they never play before you scroll to them.
  Gallery photos wipe in; other content fades up on scroll. All motion is off
  for people who turn on reduced motion.
